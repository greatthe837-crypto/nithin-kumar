01 — THE PROJECT

THE SCIENCE OF LISTENING

Protecting nature
begins with listening.

Wildlife acoustic monitoring uses audio recordings to study animals in their natural habitats. By analyzing calls, songs and environmental sounds, researchers can observe biodiversity while minimizing disturbance to wildlife.

This platform brings together wildlife education, acoustic research concepts and an AI-powered sound-event demonstration.
